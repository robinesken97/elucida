against PubChem's records read on 17 October 2026. A few complex structures (tetracyclines, steroids, paclitaxel,

27 October 2026, decided which compound a shared or doubtful name keeps. With Online search on, Name to Structure asks
PubChem for a name or InChIKey Octet cannot read offline. NCBI places no restrictions on the use or distribution of
PubChem data. Octet names PubChem as the source of these records.
